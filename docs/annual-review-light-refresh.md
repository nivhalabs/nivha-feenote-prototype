# Annual review service — light refresh definition

NIVHA Laboratory Services Ltd · Policy builder
Agreed 2 September 2026 · v1.0

This defines exactly what the annual review service (£60 + VAT per year, ROI reverse charge) delivers at each review, and what sits outside it. It is the internal working definition for fulfilment and the reference for any future dispute about scope.

## What each annual review includes

- **Law and guidance check** — employment, safety and data protection changes in the policy's jurisdictions since last issue: statutes and regulations, key case law, and regulator guidance (HSE, HSENI, HSA; ICO, DPC).
- **Master alignment** — where the NIVHA master template has moved on since the policy was issued, the policy is re-issued on the current master.
- **Cut-off wording check** — confirm the generic wording ("confirmed against the current published cut-off schedule of the analysing laboratory") still holds. No numeric cut-offs or detection windows are ever inserted.
- **Details reconfirmation** — policy owner, data protection contact and contact details checked with the buyer and corrected if stale.
- **Regeneration** — the policy rebuilt from the stored order answers on the latest master, carrying a new version stamp and the next review date twelve months out.
- **Change note** — a plain-English summary of what changed and why, or a statement that there were no material changes this year.
- **Mid-cycle re-issues** — a material change in the law triggers a re-issue outside the annual cycle, at no extra charge, as promised in the sales copy.
- **Delivery and record** — refreshed documents delivered by email; version, issue date and review date updated in the policy register.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Changes to the buyer's original answers or organisation (new sites, new jurisdictions, changed sector or testing arrangements) — handled as a separate order.
- Review of the buyer's own adapted or amended copy of the policy.
- Legal advice of any kind.
- Pack documents (leaflet, manager guidance, toolbox talk, sign-off sheet, contract clauses) — the service covers the policy document only, as stated in the terms of sale.
- Ad-hoc questions during the year — parked questions route to the buyer's policy owner.

## Mechanics (as built)

- The review runs on a fixed twelve-month cycle from the order date.
- About 30 days before the review date the office sends a renewal payment link from the admin reviews queue. Nothing is charged automatically.
- Payment extends the cycle twelve months from the previous due date, not the payment date.
- Unpaid by the review date, the service lapses without notice or charge; existing documents remain licensed but receive no further updates.
